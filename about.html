THE STORY OF

NAGATO

The Child Who Experienced Pain

From a war victim to the leader of Akatsuki, Nagato's journey shaped his dream of creating peace.

Who Was Nagato?

Nagato Uzumaki was a shinobi from Amegakure and a member of the Uzumaki clan. After losing his parents during the Second Shinobi World War, he experienced the cruelty of conflict at a young age. During his childhood, he awakened the legendary Rinnegan and trained under Jiraiya alongside Yahiko and Konan.

After Yahiko's death, Nagato changed his identity and became Pain, believing that humanity could only achieve peace by understanding suffering.

Nagato's Life Journey

Childhood

Nagato loses his parents during war and discovers his Rinnegan powers.

Jiraiya's Student

He trains with Jiraiya and learns the importance of friendship and peace.

Birth of Akatsuki

Along with Yahiko and Konan, Nagato creates Akatsuki to bring peace to the world.

Becoming Pain

After Yahiko's death, Nagato adopts the identity of Pain and controls the Six Paths.

Final Redemption

After meeting Naruto, Nagato understands true peace and uses his final power to revive Konoha.

Nagato's Characteristics

Intelligent

A brilliant strategist capable of controlling battles from a distance.

Broken Yet Strong

His suffering shaped his beliefs and determination.

Dream Of Peace

Even as Pain, his true goal was ending war forever.
