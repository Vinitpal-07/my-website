Who is Nagato?

Nagato Uzumaki was one of the strongest shinobi in Naruto Shippuden. Gifted with the legendary Rinnegan by Madara Uchiha, he became the feared leader of the Akatsuki under the name Pain. His dream was simple— create peace through shared suffering.
