LEGENDARY DOJUTSU

RINNEGAN

The Eye Of A God

The most powerful of the three great visual abilities, granting the user control over life, death and gravity.

The Legendary Eye

The Rinnegan is an ancient dojutsu considered to be the strongest of the Three Great Eyes. Nagato received the Rinnegan from Madara Uchiha and used its abilities through the Six Paths of Pain.

With this power, Nagato could control gravity, summon creatures, absorb chakra and even revive the dead.

Six Paths Technique

Deva Path

Controls gravitational forces using Almighty Push and Universal Pull.

Asura Path

Provides mechanical weapons and destructive body modifications.

Human Path

Allows the user to read minds and remove souls.

Animal Path

Summons powerful creatures for combat support.

Preta Path

Absorbs chakra-based attacks and ninjutsu.

Naraka Path

Uses the King of Hell to restore bodies and extract information.

Ultimate Rinnegan Abilities

Almighty Push

A powerful repulsive force capable of destroying massive areas.

Universal Pull

Creates attraction force that pulls enemies toward the user.

Planetary Devastation

Creates a gravitational sphere that traps enemies.

Revival Technique

The Rinnegan can restore life at the cost of the user's own energy.
