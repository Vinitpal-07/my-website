Pain's Philosophy

Nagato believed that humans would continue fighting because they could never truly understand each other's suffering. His solution was to create a world where everyone experienced the same pain, forcing humanity to seek peace.

Although his methods were extreme, his desire for peace came from the losses he suffered throughout his life.
