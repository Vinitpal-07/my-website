Those Who Chase Peace Through Pain

An organization of missing-nin who wanted to change the world through their own vision of peace.

The Akatsuki

Originally created by Yahiko, Nagato and Konan as a peace organization, Akatsuki changed after Yahiko's death. Under Nagato's leadership, the organization became one of the most feared groups in the shinobi world.

Their goal was to collect all Tailed Beasts and force the world into peace through fear and power.
